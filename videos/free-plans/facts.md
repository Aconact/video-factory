# Free plans, checked 28 Oct 2026 (official pages only)

## ChatGPT Free (OpenAI)
- Model: GPT-6 Luna for Free and Go. "GPT-6 in ChatGPT is powered by GPT-6 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers, and GPT-6 Luna for Free and Go tiers." https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/ (7 Oct 2026); ChatGPT release notes 7 Oct 2026; https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models
- Unlimited text chats: "For Free users, we're updating the default model to GPT-5.6 Luna and expanding access with unlimited text chats ... Limits will still apply for file uploads, images and other tools." https://openai.com/index/improving-gpt-5-6-sol-in-chatgpt/ (6 Aug 2026). TechCrunch, 6 Aug 2026: "separate limits for files, images, voice, and image generation".
- Think button on Free and Go (release notes, 14 Aug 2026).
- Voice available on Free (release notes, 8 Jul and 23 Sep 2026).
- Scheduled tasks: "Free users can create up to three active scheduled tasks" (release notes, 25 Aug 2026).
- Ads: rolling out to Free and Go users (UK, release notes 4 Jun 2026); the Free Tier FAQ says ads start in "certain countries".
- NOT used: the Free Tier FAQ and chatgpt.com/pricing still showed GPT-5.5 and a five-hour window when fetched; GPT-5.5 was retired on 14 Oct 2026, so those pages looked out of date.

## Claude Free (Anthropic) https://claude.com/pricing
- Models: Sonnet and Haiku. Opus and Fable: not on Free.
- Usage: rolling five-hour session window, "no fixed message count"; Pro gives at least 5x more usage per session.
- Included: chat on web/desktop/mobile; search the web, create files, run code; memory across conversations; connect apps and tools; Artifacts; Claude Design, Slides, Docs; voice mode; Projects (up to 5); skills.
- Not on Free: Claude Code, Research, Claude in Chrome, Opus, Fable.

## Gemini Free (Google)
- Models: "No Google AI Plan: Auto or Flash-Lite". With Auto on, harder prompts can also go to Flash or Pro; with Auto off every reply uses Flash-Lite. Argon is for AI Pro and Ultra. https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722
- Limits: "Standard limits"; refresh every 5 hours until the weekly limit; 32k-token context window without an AI plan. https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805
- Available without a plan: Canvas, Deep Research, image generation with Nano Banana 2, Audio overviews, Slide generation, Music generation, Gemini Live, Gems (subscriptions page).
- Not available without a plan: video generation, Daily brief, Tasks, Scheduled actions.
- Argon launch for Pro and Ultra: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-4-argon-availability/ (13 Oct 2026)

## Caveats
