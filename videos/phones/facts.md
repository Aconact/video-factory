# iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra — facts and sources

Compared: the two flagship slab phones (6.9-inch). Foldables (iPhone fold model, Galaxy Z Fold8) are left out on purpose.
Rule used: manufacturer claims, lab measurements and owner reports are kept apart and labelled as such on screen.
Checked: 11 Nov 2026. Prices are US launch prices.

## Launch
- iPhone 18 Pro Max on sale 18 Sep 2026. https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/iphone-18-pro-and-pro-max-review
- Galaxy S26 Ultra: pre-orders 25 Feb, on sale 11 Mar 2026. https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-unveils-galaxy-s26-series/

## Design and build
- iPhone 18 Pro Max: 163.4 x 78.0 x 8.75 mm, 249 g, aluminium, Ceramic Shield 2, IP68 (6 m, 30 min), MagSafe/Qi2 magnets, 25 W wireless. https://www.apple.com/uk/iphone-18-pro/specs/
- Weight went from 233 g (iPhone 17 Pro Max) to 249 g. https://9to5mac.com/iphone-18-pro-review/
- Galaxy S26 Ultra: 163.6 x 78.1 x 7.9 mm, 214 g, Armor Aluminum frame, IP68, S Pen built in (unchanged), 25 W wireless Qi2.2 without built-in magnets. https://m.gsmarena.com/samsung_galaxy_s26_ultra-review-2939.php ; https://www.engadget.com/mobile/smartphones/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-review-the-stealth-upgrade-140000629.html
- Water ratings quoted by TechRadar: 6 m (iPhone) vs 1.5 m (Galaxy). https://techradar.com/phones/samsung-galaxy-phones/iphone-18-pro-max-vs-samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra
- "Heavy 249-gram design" listed as a con. https://www.notebookcheck.net/Apple-iPhone-18-Pro-Max-Reviews-and-Specs.1401539.0.html

## Display
- iPhone: 6.9" OLED, 2868 x 1320, 460 ppi, 120 Hz, 1,600 nits HDR peak, 3,000 nits outdoor peak (Apple claims). https://www.apple.com/uk/iphone-18-pro/specs/
- Galaxy: 6.9" QHD+ LTPO AMOLED, 3120 x 1440, 1-120 Hz, 2,600 nits peak (Samsung claim), built-in Privacy Display. https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-unveils-galaxy-s26-series/ ; GSMArena link above
- ~460 vs ~500 ppi. https://techradar.com/phones/samsung-galaxy-phones/iphone-18-pro-max-vs-samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra
- Tom's Guide lab, peak brightness (test mode not stated by the article): iPhone 2,326 nits, Galaxy 1,806 nits. https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/i-put-the-iphone-18-pro-max-vs-galaxy-s26-ultra
  (the article text also says 2,396 for the iPhone; shown as "about 2,300")
- Privacy Display lowers effective resolution, brightness and contrast. https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/apple-iphone-18-pro-max-vs-samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra
- Display round: Galaxy (Tom's Guide face-off).

## Performance
- Geekbench 6, iPhone 18 Pro Max 4,741 single / 12,789 multi; Galaxy 3,785 / 11,563 (+25% / +10.6%). https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/iphone-18-pro-and-pro-max-review
- 3DMark Solar Bay Unlimited: 67.9 fps vs 49.37 fps. Stress-test stability: 79.4% vs 53.9%. Same source.
- Geekbench AI: 71,974 (iPhone) vs 83,047 (Galaxy), Galaxy +15%. Same source.
- A20 Pro CPU 22-27% and GPU 40-45% faster than A19 Pro; vapor chamber with 3x the surface area of the 17 Pro's (Apple says). https://9to5mac.com/iphone-18-pro-review/
- Galaxy vs S25 Ultra (Samsung): CPU +19%, GPU +24%, NPU +39%. https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-unveils-galaxy-s26-series/
- Chips: Apple A20 Pro; Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy.

## Cameras
- iPhone: 48 MP main (24 mm, f/1.48-f/4.0 variable aperture), 48 MP ultrawide, 48 MP 4x telephoto, 18 MP front, up to 40x digital zoom. https://www.apple.com/uk/iphone-18-pro/specs/
- Galaxy: 200 MP main, 50 MP ultrawide, 10 MP 3x tele, 50 MP 5x tele, 12 MP front, up to 100x digital. Samsung / GSMArena links above.
- Tom's Guide face-off: iPhone better in low light; Galaxy reads small text better at 40x; 10x "remarkably similar"; Galaxy has Horizon Lock video stabilisation.
- DXOMARK: iPhone 18 Pro (same cameras as Pro Max) overall 172, photo 171, video 176. https://dxomark.com/apple-iphone-18-pro-camera-test
- DXOMARK: Galaxy S26 Ultra overall 157, photo 160, video 153. https://www.dxomark.com/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-camera-test/

## Battery and charging
- Capacity (Apple does not publish it): iPhone 5,391 mAh (physical SIM; 5,567 mAh on eSIM-only), Galaxy 5,000 mAh. https://www.notebookcheck.net/Apple-iPhone-18-Pro-Max-Reviews-and-Specs.1401539.0.html ; TechRadar link above
- Tom's Guide battery test: 18:27 vs 16:40. Face-off link above.
- Both listed at 60 W wired, 25 W wireless (Tom's Guide table).
- Android Central, 15 minutes: iPhone 50%, Galaxy 41%. Link above.
- Tom's Guide, 30 minutes: iPhone 80%, Galaxy 77%. Face-off link above.

## Software
- iPhone: iOS 27, Siri AI (beta); Tom's Guide: less capable than Gemini or ChatGPT voice. https://www.apple.com/uk/iphone-18-pro/specs/ ; review link above
- Galaxy: One UI with Gemini, DeX, Good Lock; 7 OS upgrades and 7 years of security updates. Samsung / Android Central links above.
- Apple states no number of update years; BGR estimates about 5-6. https://www.bgr.com/2271417/how-long-iphone-18-pro-duo-software-support/
- Software and AI round: Galaxy (Tom's Guide face-off).

## Price (US launch)
- iPhone 18 Pro Max: $1,299 / $1,499 / $1,899 / $2,499 (256 GB / 512 GB / 1 TB / 2 TB); $100 higher than last year. https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/iphone-18-pro-and-pro-max-review ; 9to5Mac ; TechRadar
- Galaxy S26 Ultra: $1,299.99 / $1,499.99 / $1,799.99 (256 GB / 512 GB / 1 TB). TechRadar link above.

## iPhone problems
- CONFIRMED by Apple, 3 Oct 2026: some iPhone 18 Pro Max units lose AT&T cellular service; affected phones need hardware replacement; iOS 27.0.1 and a carrier update only help prevent it. https://www.macworld.com/article/3250214/apple-confirms-iphone-cellular-issue-admits-affected-units-cant-be-fixed.html
- REPORTED, not confirmed: freezes/restarts linked to Face ID and touch input on iPhone 18 Pro (Macworld could not confirm the Face ID cause). https://www.macworld.com/article/3240287/iphone-18-pro-owners-report-two-serious-bugs-causing-freezes-and-restarts.html
- REPORTED: crackling audio (1 Oct 2026); Apple has not acknowledged it; iOS 27.0.1 did not fix it. https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3248893/iphone-18-pro-audio-crackle-bug-reported.html
- Review cons: heaviest iPhone, $100 price rise, mostly incremental upgrades; 17 Pro owners have little reason to upgrade. Notebookcheck, 9to5Mac, Tom's Guide links above.
- US model is eSIM-only; no stylus. https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/apple-iphone-18-pro-max-vs-samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra

## Galaxy problems
- REPORTED by owners (Mar 2026): Privacy Display causing fuzzy text, headaches, eye strain; unclear how widespread. Samsung apologised for advertising a 10-bit panel (8-bit + 10-bit processing). https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/samsung-phones/the-galaxy-s26-ultras-headline-feature-is-turning-out-to-be-its-biggest-complaint-owners-angry-over-fuzzy-text-headaches-and-eye-strain
- REPORTED (Jul 2026): red stain on screen after 3-4 months; Samsung blamed Privacy Display in strong light at max brightness; says it can fix it. https://androidcentral.com/phones/samsung-galaxy/galaxy-s26-ultra-suffers-an-unusual-red-screen-issue-samsung-says-it-can-fix-it
- REPORTED (Apr 2026): blurry 3x telephoto; Samsung investigating. https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/samsung-galaxy/fear-not-samsung-is-investigating-the-galaxy-s26-ultras-blurry-camera
- Wireless charging often drops to 10-15 W. https://www.androidauthority.com/samsung-galaxy-s26-problems-3651083
- One UI 9: setup gets stuck after factory reset (Oct 2026). https://www.bgr.com/2300250/samsung-galaxy-users-report-battery-issue-after-one-ui-9-update/
- TechRadar review: Gemini claimed an Uber booking that did not exist. https://www.techradar.com/phones/samsung-galaxy-phones/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-review
- Review cons: unchanged S Pen, no built-in magnets, price. Engadget link above.

## Verdicts
- Tom's Guide 7-round face-off: price tie; design iPhone; display Galaxy; cameras iPhone; performance iPhone; battery and charging iPhone; software and AI Galaxy = iPhone 4, Galaxy 2, 1 tie.
- TechRadar head-to-head: iPhone 5 stars, Galaxy 4.5 stars. Android Central: iPhone winner.
- 9to5Mac: "if you're using the iPhone 17 Pro right now, there's little reason to upgrade".
